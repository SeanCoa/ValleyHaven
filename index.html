About the Property

Welcome to 79735 Fernwood Drive in Palisades Park, Covert, Michigan.

Enjoy comfortable, year-round living or the perfect vacation retreat in this beautifully maintained, fully furnished home in desirable Palisades Park.

The main living area features soaring vaulted ceilings with new skylights (2024) that fill the space with natural light. A stunning floor-to-ceiling stone veneer fireplace with an efficient wood-burning insert and a fully lined stainless steel chimney (2012) creates a warm and inviting focal point.

The open-concept kitchen offers Corian countertops, an electric range, microwave, refrigerator, and Bosch dishwasher, making meal preparation and entertaining easy. Sliding patio doors lead to a spacious wraparound deck, perfect for relaxing or gathering with family and friends.

Upstairs, the hall bathroom features a walk-in shower with a skylight, while the primary suite includes a beautifully updated en-suite bathroom (2017) with a walk-in tiled shower, tiled flooring, and a Toto toilet.

An oak-tread spiral staircase leads to the lower level, where you'll find three additional bedrooms, each with its own closet and ceiling fan. The lower level also includes a dedicated laundry room with a full-size washer and dryer, countertop workspace, and utility sink. A fully enclosed porch opens directly onto the lower-level deck, extending the living space outdoors.

The home is being sold as-is. The enclosed lower-level porch floor has experienced sagging and will require repair. The condition has been reflected in the home's pricing.

The utility room houses the hot water heater, well pressure tank, and an additional utility sink. The home is served by a private well and includes a whole-house sediment filtration system.

The attached garage features an automatic overhead door and provides ample storage for multiple golf carts. As an added bonus, the pictured E-Z-Go golf cart is included with the sale.

Heating and cooling are provided by an efficient Mitsubishi mini-split system, expanded in 2017 to serve the primary bedroom. Additional electric baseboard heat is available throughout the home, and each of the three bathrooms includes an in-wall space heater with its own thermostat for added comfort.

Additional highlights include:

  • New roof (2024)
  • LeafGuard gutter system
  • Driveway with parking for up to four vehicles
  • Spacious wraparound and lower-level decks
  • Fully furnished sale (furnishings to be finalized with purchaser)

This move-in-ready home combines thoughtful updates, comfortable living spaces, and exceptional value in one of Southwest Michigan's most desirable lake communities.
